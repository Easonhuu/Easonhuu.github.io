01 / ABOUT

A little about me

Curiosity, made practical.

I'm interested in the space where research ideas become working systems — from representation learning and language models to the careful engineering that makes them useful.

I publish under the name Yishen Hu. My public repositories include experiments in protein–drug interaction prediction, Chinese text classification, and machine learning competitions. I use this space to collect the work I can share publicly.
